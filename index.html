Content governance

Trustworthy language content, verified by the people who speak it

Indigenous-language content is the hardest part of language learning to get right — and the part most apps fake. Kengua Africa runs a formal review workflow instead of leaving it to generative AI.

Native-speaker contributionWords, phrases and audio are submitted by contributors with real fluency, not generated on demand.
Dialect & provenance trackingEvery entry stores its region, dialect and reviewer — conflicting variants coexist with an explanation instead of a silent pick.

Questions

Good to know

How is an indigenous language marked "verified"?

A native-speaker contributor submits the entry with dialect and region metadata. A reviewer approves, rejects or requests changes before it's published, and the provenance stays attached permanently.

Does the AI tutor ever invent translations?

No. The AI tutor is an assistant, not an authority — it prefers verified platform content whenever it exists, and generated material for low-resource languages is clearly labeled until reviewed.

Can I cancel a premium plan anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions can be canceled at any time and remain active until the end of the paid period.

Is my microphone recording stored?

Speaking practice only records audio after you grant explicit permission. You can replay or delete any recording, and retention is configurable and disclosed up front.
